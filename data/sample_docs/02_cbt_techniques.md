# Core CBT Techniques

## Thought Records

Thought records are a fundamental CBT tool for identifying and challenging negative thoughts.

### How to Keep a Thought Record

1. **Situation**: Describe what happened
2. **Automatic Thought**: Note your immediate thought
3. **Emotion**: Identify how you felt
4. **Evidence For**: List evidence supporting the thought
5. **Evidence Against**: List evidence contradicting the thought
6. **Alternative Thought**: Develop a more balanced perspective

## Behavioral Activation

Behavioral activation involves scheduling and engaging in positive activities to improve mood.

## Relaxation Techniques

### Deep Breathing

- Breathe in slowly through nose (4 counts)
- Hold breath (4 counts)
- Exhale slowly through mouth (6 counts)
- Repeat 5-10 times
