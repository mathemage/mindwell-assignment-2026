# Introduction to Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

## What is CBT?

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) is a structured, time-limited, problem-focused form of psychotherapy that helps people identify and change negative thought patterns that influence their emotions and behaviors.

## Core Principles

### 1. Thoughts Affect Feelings and Behaviors

Our thoughts significantly influence how we feel and behave. Negative or distorted thoughts can lead to problematic emotions and behaviors.

### 2. Skills Can Be Learned

CBT teaches practical skills that can be learned and practiced to manage thoughts, emotions, and behaviors more effectively.

## Who Can Benefit

CBT can help anyone dealing with stress, anxiety, depression, and life transitions.

It's important to note that CBT is not a replacement for medical treatment. Always consult with qualified healthcare providers for diagnosis and treatment recommendations.
